Every free calculator stops at the count

The count was never the hard part. The free calculators on this site do that. What no calculator tells you is what to actually order and what it costs you.

Supplier order units

Yards, not cubic feet. Bundles, not shingles. Pallets, not square feet. Rounded up to the increment your yard actually sells and raised to their delivery minimum.

Your prices, with a date

Enter your costs once with the order multiple and delivery minimum. Every price carries the day you set it, and goes orange when it is stale, so you never bid off last spring's number.

Labor from production rates

Hours come from quantity divided by a rate you set and adjust as you learn your crew. Not from a feeling on the drive home.

The compaction allowance

Gravel loses roughly a fifth of its loose depth when you compact it. Order by finished depth without allowing for that and you find out with the plate already on site.

A proposal, not a number

Your logo, the scope, the exclusions, an expiry date and a material escalation clause. Print it itemized, grouped, or as a single number depending on the client.

Nothing leaves your device

No server, no account, no analytics, no network call of any kind. Open the file in a text editor and search for "http" if you want to check. You will not find one.

The expensive mistake

A 55% markup is not a 55% margin

Markup is a percentage of your cost. Margin is a percentage of the price you charge. Same number in the box, and on a job that costs you $100 they are $67 apart.

Takeoff Desk shows both at once while you price the job, so you find out before you send the proposal rather than when the year closes.

Straight answers

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment, one file, yours. There is no account to keep, nothing to renew, and no tier that unlocks the feature you actually needed.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, that is the point. It is a single HTML file on your own device. Once it is downloaded there is no server involved, so it runs in a basement, a crawlspace, or a lot with no signal.

What happens to my price book if I stop paying?

Nothing, because there is nothing to stop paying. You bought the file. It stays on your device and keeps working.

Which trades does it cover?

Twelve. Concrete, drywall, paint, epoxy, gravel, mulch, sod, pavers, tile, insulation, roofing and fence. Each with its own waste factor, its own order units and the formula printed under the result.

Is it a replacement for Jobber or ServiceTitan?

No. Those are field service platforms with scheduling, dispatch, payments and a CRM. This does takeoff, estimating and the proposal document, and nothing else. If you need dispatch, buy dispatch.
